1. Introduction

Welcome to games developed by Deep Cody Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data of our players when enjoying our free mobile games.

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games published by Deep Cody Studio on Google Play and other distribution channels.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our games are designed to be played immediately without user registration:

  • Deep Cody Studio does not directly collect personal identifying information (such as your full name, phone number, physical address, email, or payment credentials).
  • We do not operate backend servers that store personal data. Gameplay progress, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide our games free of charge, we may display in-game advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-identifying technical data from your device to serve and measure advertising performance, including:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android devices.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, system language, carrier.
  • Coarse Location Data: Approximate country or city level location derived from IP address.
  • Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and engagement metrics.

For more detailed information on how Google collects, uses, and safeguards data, please review Google's advertising technologies policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Anonymous Product Analytics (PostHog)

To analyze player retention (D1/D7) and ensure balanced level difficulty progression, we use PostHog for product analytics.

PostHog records anonymous gameplay events (such as session start, level start/completion, moves taken, time spent, puzzle picture reveals) associated with an anonymous random identifier (GUID) stored locally on your device.

  • We do not collect or transmit personal names, emails, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS locations, or Advertising IDs through this analytics service.
  • All analytics data is used strictly for internal gameplay balancing and technical reliability improvements.

5. Managing and Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device:

  • Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  • Navigate to Google → Ads.
  • Toggle on Opt out of Ads Personalization, or choose Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy

Our games are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or business changes. Any updates will take effect upon publication on this page.
